Practise by domain

Pick one or more domains. Every answer is marked on the spot, and a wrong answer opens the coaching panel.

Full mock exam

60 items drawn to the blueprint weights: 16 from Domain 1, 11 from Domain 2, 12 each from Domains 3 and 4, and 9 from Domain 5. Questions lean toward four randomly chosen scenarios, the way the real exam draws a subset of the six. The clock runs only while the exam is open, and pausing saves your place.

Turn this off for exam conditions: no feedback until you finish, then a full review of every miss.

Weights and item counts

Generate fresh questions

Claude writes new scenario questions from a summary of the course material for the domain you pick. Each one arrives with the same coaching: an everyday example, a technical example and a full explanation. Generated questions are saved with your progress and marked as not reviewed, so treat a surprising answer as something to check against the study notes.

Your generated questions

Listen

One episode per domain, narrated end to end. Each part covers one task statement, so you can listen to the part that matches your weakest area. Playback carries on while you switch to practice, and each part remembers where you stopped.

Glossary

Every key term in the question bank, in plain English. Terms that turn up across three or more domains are grouped first as course-wide vocabulary; the rest sit under the domain and task statement where the questions use them most. Switch to A–Z for one alphabetical list, or search either view.

Your progress

Accuracy by domain against a 75% line. The pass mark is a scaled 720, which cannot be converted exactly from a raw score, so treat three quarters correct per domain as the working target. Any domain under 70% is a repair target whatever your total.
