Claude Foundations Prep is a focused learning site for the Claude Certified Associate - Foundations (CCAO-F) and Claude Certified Architect - Foundations (CCAR-F) exams.

Use the Study Hub to search question banks, filter by domain, and review answer rationales. The Quiz Hub provides complete CCAO-F and CCAR-F practice runs with untimed and official-guide-paced timed modes. For deeper CCAR-F preparation, the Intensive Training area combines 35 podcast episodes, five domain note guides, and a 120-question assessment.

All content is organized for deliberate practice: learn the concept, test your reasoning, and use the explanations to identify what to revisit.
