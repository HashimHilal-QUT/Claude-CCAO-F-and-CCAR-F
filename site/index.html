Claude Foundations Prep is a focused learning site for the Claude Certified Associate - Foundations (CCAO-F), Claude Certified Architect - Foundations (CCAR-F), and UD-CCAR-F Udemy practice exams.

Use the Study Hub to search question banks, filter by domain or practice test, and review answer rationales. The Quiz Hub provides complete practice runs with single- and multi-answer evaluation plus official-guide-paced timing. For deeper CCAR-F preparation, the Intensive Training area combines 35 podcast episodes, five domain note guides, and a 120-question assessment.

All content is organized for deliberate practice: learn the concept, test your reasoning, and use the explanations to identify what to revisit.
